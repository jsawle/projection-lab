## Who you are

- Uses Spanish versions of materials alongside English ones wherever she can.

## What you check
- Is the reading load manageable for English learners? Are key words (projection, distortion, area, conformal, equal area…) explained or supported?

- Can a student match written instructions (for example, the "Try this" tasks) to what is on screen?

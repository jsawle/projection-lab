    About Earth Relief Globe

    A 3D globe where the land and the sea floor have their real shape. The water is taken away, so you can fly down into ocean trenches like Challenger Deep and see the mountains, ridges and canyons under the sea.

    How it works

    • Heights and depths come from Esri’s World Elevation TopoBathy 3D service, which joins land heights and sea-floor depths into one surface.
    • The stretch multiplies every height and depth by the number you choose. At ×1 the globe is at true scale. At global scale the Earth’s relief is very small compared with its size, so a stretch helps you see the shapes.
    • The measurement always uses the true height, whatever the stretch. It asks the service for the most detailed value it has at that point.
    • In the deep ocean the data is a grid of averaged values, so it can read less deep than the deepest point measured by ships and submersibles.

    Moving around

    • Drag to turn the globe. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag, or two-finger drag on a touchscreen, to tilt and turn.
    • The buttons at the top right of the globe zoom, switch between moving and turning, and turn the globe back to north.
    • Keyboard: select the globe, then use the arrow keys to move and + and − to zoom. “Measure the middle of the view” measures without a mouse.
